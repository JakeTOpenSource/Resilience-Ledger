What is this?

Delta Atlas is a function-first glossary of the language used in agentic AI and its governance. Every term is defined by what it does, not by where it falls in an alphabet, and linked to the others by shared purpose. Underneath, a grounding method called State-Delta keeps each definition tied to how these systems actually behave, not how they are marketed.

Why not just ask a chatbot?

You can, and for many things a chatbot is great. But it rebuilds its answer from scratch every time, and the more you pile into one chat, the more it can drift: contradict itself, blur old and new, and sound sure while losing the thread.

Delta Atlas works the other way. Each term has one small, fixed, sourced definition that does not change between visits. Ask the same thing twice and you get the same answer. It stays clear because it carries less, not more. That is the whole method: keep what is true, drop the noise, and check new information before trusting it. Simplifying on purpose is what keeps a system, or a chat, from getting sloppy.

Why?

The field invents words faster than most people can learn them, and that gap quietly decides who gets to take part. Someone entering the AI workforce should be able to find out what "ontology gate," "guardrail," or "excessive agency" mean without a course and without spin.

This is open research: free to read, check, and build on. Helping people understand the work is part of doing the work.

What this doesn't do

Where it sits: this is the coherence check that runs before the enforcement layer, before you optimize, gate, or audit against your rules. It doesn't bind anything. It catches the incoherent spec before something else has to.

It does not certify anything. The groundedness score tells you how well-sourced a term is, not whether it is true.

It is not legal, compliance, or professional advice, and it is not trying to out-dictionary the dictionary. It defines what matters for understanding agentic AI and leaves the rest out on purpose.

What's next

All 439 terms are now human-reviewed, so the candidate backlog is cleared. Next up is per-term source quotes so every definition shows its receipt, more independent sources like the EU AI Act and ISO 23894, and learning paths that connect job roles to the terms they need.
