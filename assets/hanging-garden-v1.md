# Hanging-garden asset

Generated with OpenAI image generation on 2026-09-05 for Delta Atlas, following the owner's approved visual direction. An earlier owner-provided BespokeNode garden image was used as an atmosphere/material reference. The new composition depicts cream-edged ivy, ferns, small white/lilac flowers, weathered limestone and clear cascading water. It contains no factual diagram or claimed physical system.

The served `hanging-garden-v1.webp` is 1672 x 941 native pixels, 597,704 bytes. It was converted from the retained generated PNG without resizing. This is not a 4K asset. Its SHA-256 is recorded with the publication receipt.

`garden-water-v1.js` adds optional image refraction/highlights over water regions. The still image is independently rendered beneath the canvas. `garden-home-v1.css` keeps foreground content readable and shows the complete landscape above the content on narrow screens. The decoration is not connected to tool results, authority, evidence acceptance or user data.

## Pool flow, 2026-09-25 (water script v1.1)

The owner reported that the pool the waterfall empties into read as stagnant. The pool section of `garden-water-v1.js` now moves along a flow field: water arrives at the foot of the lower fall, spreads outward through the splash, then drains toward the foreground-left where the pool leaves the frame; the thin upper basin slides toward its spill lips. Two short downstream passes carry the photographed foam and reflections along that field and cross-fade so nothing snaps. Bubbles near the drop-off drift outward and catch light; open water shows faint streaks along the current. The falls, the masks, the still image and the motion lifecycle (pause, reduced motion, hidden page, tool open, context loss) are unchanged. Speeds are image-space art speeds, not physical water.
